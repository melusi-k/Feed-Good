# Feed Good — Product Brief

## Product vision

Feed Good is a gentle, practical gardening companion for people who want to care for their plants with more confidence and enjoy the relationship they build with them.

It should feel like a supportive companion, not a demanding task manager.

## Problem

Plant owners can forget care routines, feel uncertain about what a plant needs, and lose sight of the small signs of progress that make growing rewarding.

## Product promise

Help people remember the care, learn as they go, and celebrate the journey of every plant.

## Product pillars

1. **Care with confidence:** make the next useful action clear without pretending every plant follows the same schedule.
2. **Personal connection:** give every plant an identity and a history.
3. **Small moments matter:** capture new leaves, recovery, flowering and other milestones.
4. **Encouragement over guilt:** reminders and mantras should be kind, useful and easy to act on.

## Initial audience

People who keep houseplants or are beginning their gardening journey. The experience should be approachable for beginners while remaining useful to people with an established collection.

## MVP outcomes

- A person can add, view, edit and remove a plant.
- A person can record a care activity against the correct plant.
- A person can understand what care is due and why.
- A person can opt into local reminders and control them.
- A person can add a dated note or photo to a plant's journal.
- A daily mantra or care tip gives the home experience a distinctive, encouraging voice.

## Product non-goals for the first release

- Social feeds, public profiles or community messaging.
- AI plant diagnosis or guaranteed species identification.
- Mandatory accounts, cloud sync or subscriptions.
- Complex horticultural automation or unsupported claims about plant needs.

## Product principles

- Local-first and useful without an account.
- User-controlled notifications and data.
- Plant-specific schedules; avoid universal watering assumptions.
- Clear distinction between general guidance and verified plant-specific advice.
- Accessible, calm and readable interface.
- Preserve user-authored journal content and care history.

## Open product decisions

These are deliberately not assumed to be settled:
- Whether onboarding includes a curated plant catalogue or manual entry first.
- Whether care schedules are interval-based, calendar-based, or both.
- Whether the daily mantra is bundled content or remotely updated.
- Whether photo storage and export need a dedicated archive in v1.
